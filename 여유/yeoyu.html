Journal

Why 여유 Matters in NYC

Spring 2026

New York City is often defined by movement. People are constantly commuting, working, studying, and rushing from one place to the next. In that kind of environment, it can be difficult to find a space that allows people to slow down without feeling like they are wasting time.

YEO:YU is inspired by the Korean concept of 여유, which can mean leisure, ease, or having room in time and mind. For this café, 여유 is not only about relaxation; it is about creating a space where people can feel comfortable staying, working, and being around others without pressure.

The café is designed to be a third space, somewhere between home and work. During the day, guests can sit, study, read, or work independently. At the same time, shared seating and a central conversation area encourage quiet connection and presence. In the evening, the space can shift into small events such as readings, casual discussions, or community gatherings. On the weekends, we will implement a strict no laptops and WiFi rule for our guests.

Rather than focusing on speed or turnover, YEO:YU values lingering. It is a place for unhurried time, shared comfort, and small moments of connection within the pace of the city.
